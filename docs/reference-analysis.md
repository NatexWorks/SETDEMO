## Inspection limitations

- Orano's source experience did not render for direct browser inspection. Interaction notes are based on a supporting studio case description, not a fresh live-site test.
- Corn Revolution returned an unsupported-browser message. The Awwwards listing and supplied visual are supporting evidence only; exact original interactions were not reproduced or tested.
- 3D Web Lab and Christen Dijoux did not return readable pages during direct inspection. Available visual/search evidence is insufficient to claim specific scroll mechanics.
- The other three references exposed enough page structure to document visible information hierarchy. This inspection did not establish their internal framework or exact runtime implementation.

## Principles derived from the research

1. Use a few distinct interaction systems rather than one reveal animation everywhere.
2. Keep all meaning in semantic page content; visual layers only reinforce it.
3. Reserve pinned scrolling for short explanatory sequences on larger viewports.
4. Keep touch devices in natural document flow; no device rotation or hover-only requirements.
5. Use project-specific visual compositions rather than generic 3D cards.
6. Do not borrow another studio's claims, metrics, names, or brand identity.
